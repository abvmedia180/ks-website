Why We Exist

For years, people have asked us the same question: “Where should I give, so it actually helps someone?” We never had a good answer. So we built one.

Our board members have spent years supporting charitable causes, and watching too much of that giving get lost along the way. Mismanaged funds. Unclear impact. Little accountability for how donations were used. After enough of those experiences, the answer became simple: if you truly want to make a difference, sometimes you have to do it yourself.

As Christians, we believe everything we have is a gift from God, and that we are called to give generously. But Scripture also calls us to be good stewards, and generosity without accountability is incomplete. Kind Steward exists to close that gap: every dollar, every resource, and every hour of effort directed with purpose, transparency, and care.

And underneath it all, one question guides everything we fund. Not how much was raised. Not what was built. How many people were truly helped, impacted, and touched. That is the measure we hold ourselves to, and the one we will answer every year, in the open, with real numbers.

What We Do

Direct humanitarian assistance in East Africa and the United States.

Our primary focus is funding life changing medical care in Ethiopia and Kenya, working alongside trusted partners on the ground. We also support clean water and community projects in the region, and partner with churches, charities, and good causes here at home.

Primary Focus

Medical Care

Funding life changing medical care and corrective surgeries in Ethiopia and Kenya for individuals who would otherwise go without treatment.

Community Development

Supporting initiatives in East Africa including clean water infrastructure, wells, and other projects driven by the needs of local communities.

Domestic Assistance

Partnering with churches, charities, and good causes in need across the United States, with a focus on New York, Texas, and Florida.

Our Commitments

01

Every dollar, with purpose

We strive to send 100% of public donations to program. Our founders cover the operating costs, so your gift funds the work itself.

02

Zero compensation

No director or officer is paid. No salaries, no honorariums, nothing. It is written into our bylaws.

03

We show our work

Every dollar traces from donor to beneficiary, with photographic and video evidence from the field. Everything is on the books.

04

An annual letter, every year

Real numbers, once a year: people helped, dollars deployed, exactly what was accomplished. Honest, specific, and signed.

The Annual Letter

We would rather show you the work than ask you for money.

Every year, Kind Steward publishes an annual letter with specific numbers, not vague impact language. Read it in ten minutes and know exactly what was accomplished and where every dollar went. If the work speaks to you, we would be honored to have your support.

Why We Exist

For years, people have asked us the same question:

“Where should I donate, so it actually helps someone?”

We never had a good answer.

For years, we gave the way most people do: tithes, donations, checks written in good faith. Write the check, check the box, move on. At least we gave. At least we did our part. That is what we told ourselves. But would God say the same? Too often, the money disappeared into organizations that could not tell us what it accomplished.

Mismanaged funds. Unclear impact. Little accountability for how donations were used. After enough of those experiences, the answer became simple: if you truly want to make a difference, sometimes you have to do it yourself.

As Christians, we believe everything we have is a gift from God and that we are called to give generously. But Scripture calls us to more than generosity. It calls us to stewardship, and one day, to an account: not just how much we gave, but where it went and who it actually reached.

Most people never ask those questions about the money they donate. For years, we didn’t either. That is the whole idea behind Kind Steward: hold every dollar with purpose, transparency, and care until it reaches the person it was meant for, so that when the question comes, there is an answer.

Kind Steward is our answer. One question now guides everything we do: not how much was raised, not what was built, but how many people were truly impacted. That is the measure we hold ourselves to, and every year, we will answer for it with real numbers.

Our Commitments

01

Every dollar, with purpose

Our goal is that 100% of public donations reach the work. The private foundation carries the expenses, so your gift is free to do what you sent it to do.

02

Zero compensation

No salaries, no honorariums, nothing, for anyone. It is written into our bylaws.

03

We show our work

Every dollar is tracked from the moment you give to the moment it reaches someone. Documented, on the books, on the record.

04

An annual letter, every year

Real numbers, in writing: people helped, dollars deployed, and what comes next. Signed by us, answerable to you.
